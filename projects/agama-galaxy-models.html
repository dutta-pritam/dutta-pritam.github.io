Equilibrium Galaxy Modeling with AGAMA

2026, Astrophysics, HPC, Galactic dynamics

Equilibrium galaxy models built with AGAMA's self-consistent modeling (SCM) pipeline, feeding into GADGET-4 for N-body evolution.

The work sits on top of the underlying mathematics: the collisionless Boltzmann equation, action-angle variables, the Staeckel fudge, quasi-isothermal and double-power-law distribution functions, and multipole Poisson solvers.

I refactored the pipeline into two stages — a serial stage (run_iterate.py) that uses OpenMP to converge the SCM, and an MPI-parallel stage (run_sample.py) that handles memory-distributed particle sampling at the 10⁷-particle scale on a cluster.
