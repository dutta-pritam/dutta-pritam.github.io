MPI-Parallel Orbital Integration

2026, HPC, Galactic dynamics, MPI

MPI-parallel orbital integration using galpy, mpi4py, and HDF5, aimed at diagnosing and fixing poor parallel scaling.

Starting from an orbit-integration script originally written by João Amarante and Soumavo Ghosh, running orbits under the McMillan17 potential plus a transient spiral-arm perturbation, I found and fixed snapshot-naming collisions and a hardcoded model-string bug.

The current work is tracking down severe HDF5 collective I/O bottlenecks that were causing near-zero parallel speedup, with a target of parallelized I/O, collective HDF5 writes, and load-aware partitioning to reach better than 90% MPI efficiency at 128 cores.
